# Research 2026-11-27
Category: English-learning mobile and web app for absolute beginners whose native language is Vietnamese (pre-A1 to A2; adult everyday, work, and parent-managed kids tracks) | Sources read: 33

Framing: users are Vietnamese adults and children with no English. Main jobs: learn first words and phrases with native-language explanations, hear and say them, review so they stick, keep progress across devices, use for free or pay for premium. Existing features: none (new project); the goal already plans tracks, Vietnamese UI, device TTS, speech recognition with fallback, spaced repetition, sync, child profiles, free/premium entitlements.

Note on quotes: quotes below are short paraphrases or fragments from the cited page, not copied text. Where a page shows no date, "accessed 2026-11-27" is given.

## Needs
1. Subscription that is honest and easy to manage: clear trial terms, in-app place to cancel, premium appears right after paying, purchases restore after reinstall. Status: missing. Evidence: 4 sources.
   - https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/elsaspeak.com (2026-11-14, 2026-10-24, 2026-05-07): hard to cancel, charges continued; paid yearly but account reverted to free; cancellation hard to find after a trial.
   - https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/duolingo.com (2026-11-23): charged about 85 GBP after a trial the reviewer believed was free.
   - https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id930331514 (accessed 2026-11-27, via search snippet): kids app parent "ended up with two subscriptions"; paid monthly but app "still only wanted to give us the free content"; Vietnamese reviewer could not restore a valid purchase after reinstalling.
   - https://nubiapage.com/elsa-speak-review-2026/ (2026): after buying premium "account still displays the free version".
2. Learning progress and streaks are never lost (reinstall, re-login, bugs). Status: missing. Evidence: 3 sources, severe (data loss).
   - https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/id930331514 (2019-09-27): after reopening, login reset and months of lesson progress disappeared; support could not recover it.
   - https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/elsaspeak.com (2026-07-04): a 400+ day streak lost to a bug, support refused to restore.
   - https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/elsa-speak-h%E1%BB%8Dc-n%C3%B3i-ti%E1%BA%BFng-anh/id1083804886?l=vi (2017-12-12, old): scores did not update and progress did not sync.
3. Clear grammar and usage explanations in the learner's own language, not just guess-and-correct. Status: missing. Evidence: 5 sources.
   - https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/duolingo.com (2026-11-25): lessons lack grammar explanations, reviewer feels they learn little.
   - https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=33928446 (2022-12-10): wishes for "much more formal information on the grammatical rules".
   - https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44857656 (2025-08-10): regrets removal of the explanation section.
   - https://learnenglish.life/resources/best-english-app-for-vietnamese-speakers/ (updated 2026-10): Vietnamese speakers need explanations in Vietnamese; no tenses or articles in Vietnamese makes these hard.
   - https://migaku.com/blog/language-fun/duolingo-vietnamese-review (accessed 2026-11-27): grammar explanations disappear after beginner lessons.
4. A free plan that lets you actually learn every day (not cut off after 2-3 lessons, not flooded with ads), at a price Vietnamese learners can afford. Status: missing. Evidence: 5 sources.
   - https://www.androidauthority.com/quitting-duolingo-energy-system-3599842/ (2025-10-01): energy drains on every exercise; free users hit the wall around the third lesson.
   - https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/duolingo.com (2026-11-23, 2026-11-24): constant ads and daily limits on free version; ads everywhere.
   - https://www.change.org/p/duolingo-hearts-not-energy (2025, accessed 2026-11-27): petition says the system punishes correct answers.
   - https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/elsa-speak-h%E1%BB%8Dc-n%C3%B3i-ti%E1%BA%BFng-anh/id1083804886?l=vi (2021-10-05): app good but paid lessons unaffordable. Price points on the same page: 145,000 VND/month, 769,000-1,095,000 VND/year, premium 1,590,000 VND/year.
   - https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/id930331514 (accessed 2026-11-27): kids app 799,000 VND/year; bundle 1,299,000 VND/year. Vietnam top Duolingo annual tier reported at 2,990,000 VND (https://cybernews.com/vpn-guides/buy-duolingo-plans-cheaper/, 2026).
5. Pronunciation practice aimed at sounds Vietnamese speakers get wrong (final consonants, th, s/z, clusters), with scoring that is fair. Status: missing. Evidence: 4 sources.
   - https://learnenglish.life/resources/best-english-app-for-vietnamese-speakers/ (2026-10): learners drop final consonants ("cat" to "ca"), struggle with th, s/z, "str".
   - https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/elsaspeak.com (2026-07-21, 2026-09-21): AI sometimes grades pronunciation wrongly; speech recognition errors.
   - https://makeheadway.com/blog/elsa-speak-review/ (accessed 2026-11-27): AI "sometimes grades my pronunciation wrong".
   - https://github.com/kantord/LibreLingo/issues/1143 (2021-03-03, 3 votes): wants challenges for characters and their pronunciation.
6. Practical, real-life phrases and focused lessons instead of childish gimmicks and long animations (adults). Status: missing. Evidence: 4 sources.
   - https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/duolingo.com (2026-11-24): phrases taught are ones no one would say.
   - https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/duolingo.com (2026-11-21, 2026-11-23, 2026-11-22; different reviewers): too much time on animations; childish, cluttered with games and XP; gamification annoying.
   - https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=33931674 (2022-12-10): wants learning itself to be the game, not extrinsic rewards.
   - https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=36611933 (2023-07-06): audio conversation lessons were far better than generated lesson content.
7. App works reliably: no crashes, login failures or layout bugs that hide buttons. Status: missing. Evidence: 4 sources.
   - https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/elsaspeak.com (2026-11-14, 2026-06-24): crashes; unable to log in for months.
   - https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/duolingo.com (2026-11-25, 2026-10-29): layout hides the skip button; display bugs hide the screen.
   - https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/lang-kingdom/id1353744959 (accessed 2026-11-27, via search snippet): reviewer reports crashes.
   - https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/id930331514 (2020-02-22): no sound on iPad (support pointed to silent mode).
8. A daily habit/streak that motivates but is fair (does not punish, cannot be lost unfairly). Status: missing. Evidence: 3 sources.
   - https://github.com/ankitects/anki/issues/4085 (2025-06-14, 11 votes): consider a user-friendly streak.
   - https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/elsaspeak.com (2026-07-04): streak lost, rules unclear.
   - https://www.androidauthority.com/quitting-duolingo-energy-system-3599842/ (2025-10-01): author quit after a 700+ day streak.
9. Parents see each child's progress separately, several children under one plan. Status: missing. Evidence: 4 sources (product pages, market expectation).
   - https://help.lingokids.com/hc/en-us/articles/9568131412881-How-to-manage-kid-profile (accessed 2026-11-27): up to four kid profiles per subscription, separate progress.
   - https://apps.apple.com/app/id428920239 (accessed 2026-11-27): up to four profiles, individual progress tracking.
   - https://apps.apple.com/app/id6781978508 (accessed 2026-11-27): up to three child profiles with own progress; 3.99 USD/month, 29.99 USD/year.
   - https://support.splashlearn.com/hc/en-us/articles/12275178452370-How-can-parents-add-another-child-profile (accessed 2026-11-27): max 3 child profiles per account.
10. Kids' first words are basic and age-appropriate (cat, dog before grasshopper). Status: missing. Evidence: 1 source, kept only as a content guideline for the kids track (see Not pursued).

- Responsive customer support: operational, not a product feature (ELSA and Duolingo Trustpilot pages above).
- Live tutors / AI conversation partner: changes product scope and cost (learnenglish.life 2026-10; https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44934725, 2025-08-17).

- Representation of the child's ethnicity in images: one source (same page); recorded human audio and images are out of scope for now.
- Southern vs Northern accent: applies to learning Vietnamese, not English (https://www.clozemaster.com/blog/?p=7799).
