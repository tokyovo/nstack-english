# Content sources

Every unit and item names a `source` (an item without one uses its unit's). Each source id used in `content/` must have a row here; the content test (`src/content/content.test.ts`) reads the first column.

Outside data may be added only with a licence that allows commercial use, checked on the source's own page, fetched by a pinned script in the repo, with its attribution written here. Candidates and their licences are in `.nstack/research.md` ("Content sources").

| Id | What | Licence | Commercial use | Attribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `original` | Words, translations, examples, usage notes and exercises written for this app | Owned by the app's publisher | Yes | None needed |
